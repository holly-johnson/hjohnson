Orbit

Prototyping with Claude as a partner, building from the real Helios library.

A separate workspace for working through a feature with Claude as a partner across the whole effort rather than one session at a time, with the work saved between sessions so nothing had to be re-explained and a prototype as the output.

The point was what the prototype was made of. Assembled from published Helios components, it can only do what the system actually supports, so it cannot promise behavior that does not exist. When someone had to build something locally to finish an idea, that marked a gap in the system. It came back as a contribution.
