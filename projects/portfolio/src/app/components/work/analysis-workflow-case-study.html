01 / THE PROBLEM

The tool showed records. The analyst had to build the story.

Analysts were working across call records, locations, financial activity, identities, and digital communications. The information existed, but it was split across dense tables and isolated views.

Every pivot introduced a risk: filters reset, time ranges drifted, selected entities disappeared, and the reason a record mattered lived in the analyst’s memory or a separate note. The real design problem was continuity—not simply visualization.

05 / OUTCOME

A workflow analysts could follow—and explain

The redesign gave analysts a consistent path from orientation to evidence capture. Instead of rebuilding context after every pivot, they could compare different views of the same investigative frame and preserve why a finding mattered alongside its source records.

What I learned

In high-stakes analytical tools, speed is not only fewer clicks. It is fewer moments where a person has to reconstruct what they were looking at, why it mattered, and whether the evidence still supports the conclusion.
