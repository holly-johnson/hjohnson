About

I’m a product designer, systems thinker and someone who asks a lot of questions.

Getting into complicated products and figuring out how all the pieces fit together: people, workflows, technology and the teams building it. That’s the kind of work I love. It’s what pulled me toward design systems. It’s still the kind of problem I’m happiest working on.

I care about making things clearer, building things that last and sticking around long enough to learn what happens after they ship.
